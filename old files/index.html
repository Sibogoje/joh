About

BACKGROUND
Siyakhula Savings and Credit Cooperative is a savings and credit organisation founded by employees of Eswatini Water Services Corporation in 1991, incoporated in accordance to the
Cooperatives Actin Eswatini.
It was formed with the sole mission of helping its members improve their standard of living and achievements in life by providing them with savings scheme, loan schemes, financial advice/counselling and training.
VISION
 To be an innovative and leading Savings and Credit Cooperative.
MISSION STATEMENT
To provide quality, affordable and competitive savings and credit products for the betterment of
our members through their contribution.
JOINING
  •  New members’ application must be approved by the Siyakhula board.
  •  A once-off joining fee of E150.00, annual subscription of E100.00 and a minimum monthly savings of E200.00.
  •  Share capital of E3500.00 payable over 6 months.
JOINING REQUIREMENTS
  1.  Proof of Identity
  2.  Proof of income
  3.  Proof of Residence

Loans

  • All

SCHOOL LOAN

This facility is available for members who have opened a school savings account, minimum savings of E120.00. The loan offered shall be for an amount not exceeding E30 000.00 or 4 times savings accumulated by member. The Maximum repayment period shall not exceed 12 months, interest shall be 1.3% per month on a reducing balance.

ORDINARY LOAN

Loan offered at two times the member’s savings not exceeding E300 000.00. Repayment period is up to 84 months, interest on loan shall be 1.5% per month reducing balance method. Members are expected to be saving a minimum of E250.00 whilst repaying the loan.

LUNYAWO CAR LOAN

This is to provide members with access to purchase a new or second hand car. The loan application shall not exceed three times the members car saving. Interest on loan shall be 1.5% per month on a reducing balance.

EMERGENCY LOAN

Member must have contributed at least six months membership and contributing fully in share-holding. The loan offered shall not exceed E15 000.00 and shall be repaid for a period not exceeding 12 months. Interest shall be 1.75% per month on a reducing balance.

SHORT LOAN

Member must have contributed atleast six months membership and must have shares paid in full. The loan offered shall not exceed E5000.00 and shall be repaid for a period not exceeding 2 months. Interest shall be 5% per month on a reducing balance.

PHEFUMULA LOAN

Member must have contributed for atlleast six months and must have shares paid in full. The loan offered shall not exceed E5000.00 and shall be repaid for a period not exceeding 6 months. Interest shall be 3.5% per month on a compound interest.

UMLAMULELI LOAN

Maximum loan granted is E3000.00. Maximum loan repayment period is 36 months. Interest for this loan shall be 5% at reducing balance.

SAVINGS

  • All

CHRISTMAS SAVINGS

* A member is free to withdraw the savings 3 times a year.
* An interest shall be added to the member’s savings.
* Minimum Christmas savings is E50.00.

INGUNGU SAVINGS

* Minimum amount to be saved is E2 000.00.
* Rate of return is 10% per annum, subject to review.
* Fixed deposit can only be withdrawn after a period of 36 months.

FUNERAL COVER

  • All

TINYEMBETI RELIEF FUND

The purpose of this fund is to help finance members in the event of death of a member or relative.

SIYAKHULA FUNERAL SCHEME

The purpose of the scheme is to provide financial assistance towards the burial of a member of the scheme or the member’s dependants in the event of the death.
